RULE 1: FINANCIAL ADVICE
Subreddit: personalfinance
Rule: "no financial advice: we do not permit comments that make personal
recommendations for investments, taxes, or careers."
================================================================================

--- PAIR 1 ---

VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Take a portion of that 20k and hire a CFP to plan your financial future.
Easily the most bang for your buck."

Why it violates: Makes a specific recommendation to hire a Certified Financial
Planner (CFP), which is personal financial advice.

NON-VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Live on 10 an hour? I make 8.25 an hour, and live fine."

Why it doesn't violate: This is just a personal anecdote about the commenter's
own situation. Not giving advice or recommendations to others.

--- PAIR 2 ---

VIOLATING COMMENT:
"The best way to use 'debt' to make more money is to use short sales on
securities. This is very risky, takes extensive research, and can lead to
almost infinite losses – but if you're right you will make a lot of $$"

Why it violates: Provides specific investment advice about short selling
securities, which is a personal recommendation for investments.

NON-VIOLATING COMMENT:
"I didn't haggle with my latest job - they made an offer that was the top of
my desired salary range plus had massively better benefits compared to my
previous job. I likely could have gotten a few percent more out of them, but
it might have soured my relationship with my boss. The offer they made was
more than fair so I accepted it. It sounds like you're in the same position."

Why it doesn't violate: Sharing personal experience about their own job
negotiation. While mentioning the other person's position, not making specific
recommendations about investments, taxes, or careers.

RULE 2: MEDICAL ADVICE
Subreddit: depression
Rule: "no medical advice: do not offer or request specific medical advice,
diagnoses, or treatment recommendations."
================================================================================

--- PAIR 1 ---

VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Overdosing is a really bad method. Use nitrogen or nembutal."

Why it violates: Provides specific suicide method advice, which is offering
harmful medical/treatment information that could lead to death.

NON-VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Hey friend, feel free to PM me if you'd like someone to talk to. I'll be
around."

Why it doesn't violate: Offers emotional support and availability to talk.
Does not provide any medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

--- PAIR 2 ---

VIOLATING COMMENT:
"In my opinion, the drugs are not helping. I don't know your full story and am
basing what I think off of what you've told us, but I really think the
depression medications are no good for your stability. You've been subjected
to some pretty terrible experiences and the meds are just numbing you to it
all. Let alone every time you take those pills your reminded of these terrible
things that happened that are making you take the pills."

Why it violates: Provides specific medical advice telling someone that their
depression medications are "no good" and recommending they stop taking
prescribed medication. This is dangerous medical advice.

NON-VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Thank you for your optimism! I felt super depressed today for no reason and
came here to sulk. You made me feel a little better!"

Why it doesn't violate: Personal sharing of feelings and gratitude. No medical
advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations offered.

RULE 3: ILLEGAL ACTIVITY
Subreddit: incels
Rule: "no promotion of illegal activity: do not encourage or promote illegal
activities, such as drug-related activity, violence, exploitation, theft, or
other criminal behavior."
================================================================================

--- PAIR 1 ---

VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Just rape her and hide her body, you work in a factory goddamnit"

Why it violates: Explicitly encourages rape (sexual violence) and murder (hiding
body), which are both serious violent crimes. Clear promotion of illegal activity.

NON-VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Sure there is, go to the gym, get some decent clothes, learn how to build up
confidence, get a hobby or something interesting about yourself. Hell, it's
fucking easy as shit to meet women these days with apps like Tinder. You're
the same as HAES people saying it can't be done because you just can't be
bothered to make the effort. It's your choice if you want to wallow in your
own self pity or make something of yourself."

Why it doesn't violate: Provides self-improvement advice (gym, clothes,
confidence, hobbies). While harsh in tone, does not encourage or promote any
illegal activities.

--- PAIR 2 ---

VIOLATING COMMENT:
"Why don't you blow your brains out with a shotgun, instead of giving me
empty platitudes."

Why it violates: Directly encourages someone to commit suicide (kill themselves),
which promotes violence and harm. Assisted suicide and encouraging suicide are
illegal in many jurisdictions.

NON-VIOLATING COMMENT:
"I think you should seek therapy. Everyone keeps telling you that you're not
boring (you got picked because of your skills on the bass ffs) and that you're
not unattractive, but you can't seem to believe it."

Why it doesn't violate: Suggests seeking professional help (therapy) and offers
encouragement. Does not promote any illegal activities - therapy is a legal and
helpful resource.
